Power Yoga, Flexibility & Mind-Body Wellness

In today's fast-paced corporate and urban life, chronic stress, stiff spines, poor posture, and shallow breathing compromise health. The Power Yoga & Wellness Program at Kosmic Studio 4 Danz is a therapeutic blend of dynamic Vinyasa flow, Ashtanga strength building, deep myofascial stretching, and calming Pranayama meditation.

Practiced inside clean, quiet, fully air-conditioned studios with calming ambient lighting, our morning sessions help you decompress, melt away lower back pain, tone your deep core muscles, and start every day with immense mental clarity and vitality.

Key Elements of Our Wellness Sessions:

Vinyasa Flow

Surya Namaskars & Dynamic Core Sequences

Synchronizing breath with movement to build full-body internal heat, stimulate metabolism, and tone large muscle groups.

Spinal Therapy

Postural Alignment & Back Pain Relief

Targeted asanas to correct rounded shoulders, anterior pelvic tilt, sciatica issues, and neck stiffness caused by desk jobs.

Breath & Mind

Pranayama, Meditation & Deep Savasana

Anulom Vilom, Kapalbhati, and guided mindfulness meditation that lower resting heart rates and reduce anxiety.

Terms & Studio Policies

1. Studio Etiquette & Attire

Members must wear clean indoor dance sneakers or jazz shoes inside the studio hall. Barefoot dancing is permitted only for classical Kuchipudi and Yoga sessions. Street footwear is strictly prohibited on the wooden dance floors.

2. Batch Timings & Punctuality

Warm-up is crucial to prevent injury. Students arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to sit out the initial high-intensity choreography to avoid muscular strain.

3. Fees & Membership Validity

All monthly and quarterly passes are valid for the calendar duration purchased. Missed classes can be made up within the same month across alternative batch slots with prior instructor notice.

4. Safety & Medical Disclosure

Participants joining the 5-Days-5-Variation Weight Loss or high-intensity Zumba programs must disclose any pre-existing medical conditions, past injuries, or cardiovascular alerts to Master Mahesh Babu prior to commencement.
